# Civic Issue - Citizen User Guide

## Overview
Civic Issue allows citizens to report, track, and follow up on local problems such as roads, sanitation, lighting, and public infrastructure issues.

## Creating a Report

1. Sign in as a citizen.
2. Select **Report Issue**.
3. Choose the issue category.
4. Add a clear description.
5. Attach photos or record a voice note.
6. Confirm the location.
7. Review the details and submit.

## Voice Reports

- Use the voice recorder to describe the issue.
- Speech transcription may help fill the description.
- Audio is attached as evidence and does not replace your written description.

## Offline Reporting

Reports can be created without an internet connection.

- Submit reports while offline.
- They are stored as pending.
- They automatically sync when connectivity returns.
- Use the pending sync indicator to check queued reports.

## Tracking Reports

Open **My Reports** to view progress.

Statuses include:
- Submitted
- Acknowledged
- Assigned
- In Progress
- Resolved

- Citizen Approved (where enabled)

## Good Reporting Practices

- Provide accurate locations.
- Include useful descriptions.
- Upload evidence when available.
- Avoid duplicate reports for the same issue.

## Resolution Feedback

After an issue is marked resolved, review the provided information and give feedback when requested.
